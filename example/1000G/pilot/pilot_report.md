Two things to read off these numbers. HG00732 has lost an X chromosome in part of its culture - 1.61 copies in the 2019 DNA, 1.84 in the 2015 DNA - so the two DNA batches of a cell line are not the same biological sample, and disagreement between libraries bounds measurement error from above. And the NYGC libraries read both the female X (1.91-1.98) and the DJ (9.5-9.9) a few percent low while the older libraries read them near 2 and 10: the inactive X and the acrocentric short arms replicate late, and DNA from a culture with more cells in S phase under-represents late-replicating sequence. That is a hypothesis, not a result; the cohort can test it (section 9 of DESIGN.md).

### Window by window

d = log(C_replicate / C_NYGC) per 250-bp window of the 45S unit, each library under its own GC model, 12 pairs. Over the 117 moderate-GC (40-60%) windows the median d ranges from -1.43 to +0.31, yet for a given window it is nearly the same in every individual (median 10-90% spread across pairs 0.15): the disagreement is a deterministic property of sequence x chemistry - dropout zones one fragment wide around particular elements, strand-asymmetric at fine scale - not noise, and not something a genome-wide GC curve can carry.

**Consensus windows** - moderate GC, |median d| < 0.06, 10-90% spread < 0.15 - are windows on which the two chemistries agree. They were learned with one family left out; each library was then calibrated separately (its own window efficiencies) with those windows as the anchor, and the left-out family's pairs compared. The cross-library level is therefore out of sample:

## 5. chrY, and the state of the culture

chrY (40 X-degenerate regions; truth 1 or 0), NYGC: males 0.979 (SD 0.004, range 0.974-0.985), females 0.0021 (max 0.0029). Older libraries: males 0.979, females 0.0020.

Mitochondrial genomes and EBV episomes per cell are not truths but covariates: they describe the culture the DNA came from. A look, not a test: the cohort has the trios to ask whether a child's departure from the midparent tracks the state of its culture.
